Guide Sans: renamed Latin subset of Arimo, a metrically compatible alternative to Arial.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/arimo

Guide Serif: renamed Latin subset of Gelasio, a metrically compatible alternative to Georgia.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/gelasio

Both font families are distributed under their included SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright and license name records are preserved. Subsets include Latin, Portuguese/Spanish accents, punctuation, currency signs and arrows.
