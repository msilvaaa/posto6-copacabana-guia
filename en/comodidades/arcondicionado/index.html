Air conditioning

How to use

  • Turn off both the bedroom and living-room air conditioners whenever you leave the apartment. With nobody home to notice a fault, an equipment problem can increase the risk of fire.
  • The appliances cool the rooms quickly when you return. If they remain on while you are away, I receive notifications to turn them off through the app.
  • To turn off the appliance light at night, press LIGHT OFF on the remote control.
