Dishwasher

How to use

  • Use products intended specifically for dishwashers. Do not use ordinary dish soap or laundry detergent.
  • The appliance is kept switched off to prevent unsuitable products being used. Contact me so I can activate it remotely.
  • Use a dishwasher tablet and rinse aid. The products shown are FINISH TABS and CIF rinse aid.
