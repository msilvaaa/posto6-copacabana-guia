Trash

Tras Upon leaving the apartment to your right, across from the service elevator, you will encounter the building's staircase. The trash can is situated on the staircase, opposite the service elevator. For items like bottles, glass, sharp objects, and organic food, the building staff will ring the doorbells of all apartments daily at 4:00 PM to gather these materials. If you prefer not to be disturbed, please notify them that ringing the doorbell for this purpose is unnecessary. Collection of materials that are not permitted to be disposed of in the building's trash can. On the ground floor of the building, you will find a yellow/orange trash can designated for the disposal of bottles, glass, sharp objects, and organic food waste. If you have missed the 4:00 PM disposal time or would rather not be interrupted, please use this receptacle. Kindly refrain from leaving litter in the stairwells of the building.
