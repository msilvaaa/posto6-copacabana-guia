Trash and recycling

How to use

  • Leave the apartment and turn right toward the stairs opposite the service elevator. The trash disposal is on that staircase.
  • Building staff collect bottles, glass, sharp objects and organic waste daily at 4 pm, ringing the apartment bells. Tell them if you prefer not to be disturbed.
  • If you miss collection or prefer to dispose of these materials directly, use the yellow/orange bin on the ground floor.
  • Do not leave rubbish in the building stairwells.
