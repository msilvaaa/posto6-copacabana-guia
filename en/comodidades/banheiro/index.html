Bathroom

How to use

  • Do not adjust the main water valve, marked 1 in the photo.
  • For hot water, turn control 2 to the right and wait about 10 seconds.
  • Turn control 3 to the left for the hand shower. Turn control 4 to the left for cold water.
  • At the basin tap, follow the movements shown in the photos to select hot or cold water.
