House rules

Thank you for following the house rules

  1. 01

    Turn off the air conditioning whenever you leave the apartment.

  2. 02

    Observe quiet hours from 10 pm to 9 am.

  3. 03

    Pets are not allowed.

  4. 04

    Do not invite additional guests without prior approval.

  5. 05

    Smoking is prohibited inside the apartment.

  6. 06

    Report any damage during your stay immediately.

Guests and visitors

Additional guests are subject to a charge of R$280 per guest, per night. Visitors not registered with the building must be reported.
